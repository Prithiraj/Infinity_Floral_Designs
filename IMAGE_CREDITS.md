# Image Credits & Rights Notes

The current site is a **design prototype**. The photographs below are real floral photographs selected for visual direction, but they are **not presented as photographs of Infinity Floral Designs, its team, its current shop, its products, or its portfolio**.

The three user-supplied floral website concept images are used **only as visual-design references**. Their depicted businesses, arrangements, prices, reviews, locations, hours, telephone numbers, delivery promises, services, and other sample content are not reused as factual business content and the reference images themselves are not published as site photography.

Unsplash states that standard-license images can generally be used for commercial and noncommercial projects. Third-party rights can still matter for recognizable people, private property, brands, or copyrighted works depicted in an image. See:

- https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612315-can-i-use-unsplash-images-for-personal-or-commercial-projects
- https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612329-releases-and-trademarks

## Replacement priority before commercial launch

Replace stock photography in this order when owner-controlled assets become available:

1. Hero image.
2. Current storefront / studio.
3. Real arrangements made by the business.
4. Florist/team/process photography.
5. Delivery/pickup imagery, if actually offered.
6. Current social-proof imagery, only with permission.

Do not substitute competitor imagery, Google Maps contributor photos, review-platform imagery, or photos belonging to the unrelated Arizona business using the same name without clear commercial rights.
